The Jaeger family, since 1979

We were Michigan student tenants ourselves. Our first rental house was filled with fellow students, and more than 45 years later the philosophy hasn't changed:

"We would gladly live in any one of our properties, because it's just like home."

Both our daughters are Michigan graduates, and we hold every home to the standard they lived in: modern kitchens and baths, quality furnishings, motion-lit parking, well-maintained locks, fire extinguishers by every stove, and energy-efficient appliances throughout.

When a new group moves in, the home has been cleaned top to bottom, painted, and the carpets professionally cleaned. Many of our tenants are siblings and friends of past tenants, and that's exactly how we like it.

Eric & Mary Anne Jaeger
